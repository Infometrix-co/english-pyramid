7. What we got wrong

Six beliefs we tested, and lost

  • “Away form separates promotion from relegation more than home form does.”

    We tested it seven ways. Six found nothing. For sides that went up, home form was the sharper measure in every division; the one detection was Premier League relegation. Mixed, no story.

    Evidence

    data/pr15_asymmetry.json, tested 2026-08-20

  • “A side coming up from the National League needs 61 points to stay up.”

    Hartlepool survived in 2021 with 54 points and finished 17th.

    Evidence

    data/pr2_extended_cohorts.json, tested 2026-08-21

  • “Sides already doomed fade in the run-in.”

    We tested it twelve ways. None held; pooled p = .41.

    Evidence

    data/pr3_pace_fade.json, tested 2026-08-22

  • “Pass completion sorts winners from losers inside every division.”

    Sometimes it did, especially in the Championship. But it did not hold everywhere: League Two relegation broke the claim.

    Evidence

    docs/precision/precision_results.json, tested 2026-08-24

  • “In every division, at least one relegated side passed better than at least one champion.”

    The Championship killed it by 0.06 passes in a hundred: Leicester 25/26 at 81.38, Coventry 25/26 at 81.44. Our own build gate caught it before a reader did.

    Evidence

    build gate G1, commit aaceced, tested 2026-08-26

  • “You cannot win the league away from home, but you can lose it there.”

    The sides that went up were the ones taking results away from home, in all 3 divisions where it could be tested. The saying had it backwards.

    Evidence

    data/pr1_asymmetry.json, tested 2026-08-26
