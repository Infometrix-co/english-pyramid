2023/24 to 2025/26: 20 clubs, 5 divisions

  • Five divisions, the Premier League at the top and the National League at the bottom, 2023/24 to 2025/26, then where the last season's moves lead.
  • Every move is a grey line.
  • 20 clubs are followed in their own colour and by name: Arsenal, Birmingham, Bromley, Burnley, Burton Albion, Cambridge United, Cardiff, Carlisle, Crawley Town, Grimsby, Ipswich, Leicester, Luton, Manchester City, Morecambe, Oxford United, Port Vale, Rotherham, Southampton, Wrexham.
  • On a phone, the names told longest ago give way to a dot in the club's colour.
  • The stories, in order:
    1. Burnley, promoted to the Premier League
    2. Luton, relegated to League One
    3. Birmingham, promoted to the Championship
    4. Wrexham, promoted to the Championship
    5. Port Vale, promoted to League One
    6. Carlisle, relegated to the National League
    7. Ipswich, promoted to the Premier League
    8. Leicester, relegated to League One
    9. Cardiff, promoted to the Championship
    10. Cambridge United, promoted to League One
    11. Morecambe, to tier 6
    12. Grimsby, stayed in League Two
  • 12 of 48 moves between divisions were reversed the very next season.
  • Higher in a band means finished higher that season.
  • The Next season column shows where 2025/26's moves lead, not a league table.
